Privacy Policy — gooner-threads-ops

Last updated: October 29, 2026 ／ 日本語は下にあります。

Who we are

gooner-threads-ops is an internal tool operated by the owner of the Threads account @mushano_career (Takuya Mushano, career advisor). It is not offered to the public; the only user who signs in is the operator of that account.

Data we access through the Threads API

  • Our own account: profile, our own posts, replies to our posts and post insights (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes) — to publish our posts and measure their performance.
  • Public posts found by keyword search (threads_keyword_search): text, media type, permalink, timestamp and username of public posts — to research which topics people in construction management and job-change discuss, so we can write useful posts.
  • Public profiles (threads_profile_discovery): username, display name, biography, follower and engagement counts, verification status of public accounts — to compare our account with similar public accounts.

We only read data that Threads already shows publicly or that belongs to our own account. We do not access private profiles, direct messages, or contact information.

How we use and store it

  • Results are used only by the operator for content planning and benchmarking. Notes derived from them are stored in the operator's private files; we do not build profiles of individual people.
  • We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or for contacting the people who wrote the posts.
  • Access tokens are kept in private environment settings and are never published.
  • Raw search results are not kept longer than 90 days.

Deleting your data

If you want any data about you removed from our files, follow the steps on the data deletion page. We delete it within 30 days.

Contact

Send a direct message to @mushano_career on Threads.

日本語

gooner-threads-ops は、Threads アカウント @mushano_career（武者野拓也・キャリアアドバイザー）の運営者が使う社内ツールです。一般には提供しておらず、ログインするのは運営者本人だけです。

Threads API で扱うのは、①自分のアカウントのプロフィール・投稿・返信・投稿の数値、②キーワード検索で見つかる公開投稿（本文・種類・URL・日時・ユーザー名）、③公開プロフィール（ユーザー名・表示名・自己紹介・フォロワー数などの数値・認証の有無）です。目的は、施工管理や転職について人々が話している話題を調べて投稿の内容を考えることと、近い公開アカウントとの比較です。非公開のプロフィール・DM・連絡先には触れません。

データは運営者が投稿の企画と比較のためだけに使い、個人ごとの記録は作りません。第三者への販売・提供、広告への利用、投稿者への連絡には使いません。検索結果の生データは90日を超えて保持しません。アクセストークンは非公開の環境設定に置き、公開しません。

削除の依頼は データ削除のページ の手順でお送りください。30日以内に削除します。問い合わせは Threads の @mushano_career への DM でお願いします。
